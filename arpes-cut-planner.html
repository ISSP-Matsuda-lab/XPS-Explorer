Model & angle conventions

Free-electron final-state momentum at the Fermi level: Ekin = hν − φ. Photon momentum, band dispersion, matrix elements and intensity are not included; colors encode scan coordinates.

k₀ = √(0.2624684 Ekin) Å⁻¹ · k⊥ = √[0.2624684 (Ekin cos²s cos²d + V₀)] Å⁻¹

Deflector: (kₛ, kᵈ) = k₀ (sin s, −cos s sin d).

Offsets are subtracted from the input angles. A positive azimuth rotates the slit toward normal × slit. In 2D, the map projects onto the crystal kx–ky plane and omits kz.

Reduced units use a/π for kx (π/a), b/π for ky (π/b), and c/π for kz (π/c). The normal-emission curve also uses c/π. These are Cartesian components, not fractional reciprocal-lattice coordinates; c is a reference length for a tilted surface or a 2D lattice.

For primitive orthogonal and hexagonal lattices, Γ to the z boundary is π/c and adjacent Γ points are 2π/c apart. Centering and oblique axes can change these distances.

Use cases

Plan slit and deflector scans, compare accessible momenta with bulk and surface Brillouin zones, and estimate emission angles and photon-energy ranges before an ARPES measurement.

All occupied zones keeps the reference zone green and adds occupied neighboring zones in purple. These are translated reciprocal cells, not successively numbered Brillouin shells.

For bright colors throughout the scale, choose a palette from Bright & pastel. The map toolbar provides PNG export, zoom, pan, rotation, and reset. In zoom mode, drag upward to zoom in and downward to zoom out.

Hover guides snap to the nearest measured point and disappear away from data. In 3D they project the selected point onto the three rear axis planes. The default color scale is Rainbow, colored by scan angle.

Limitations

This is a geometric planning tool. Results depend on the entered lattice, orientation, work function, inner potential, and angle offsets. Check the instrument's angle conventions and compare with calibrated measurements and literature.

Surface symmetry labels describe the calculated surface lattice. They do not include surface reconstruction or material-specific band symmetry. Unreachable points exceed the available vacuum momentum.
